# Changelog

## v2.0.0 (2026-10-30)
- Complete re-implementation of the chloroplast regulatory-element pipeline.
- Experimentally validated reference library mapped to plastome loci.
- Statistically calibrated de novo prediction (dinucleotide-shuffle nulls, BH-FDR),
  negative/positive controls, anti-SD hybridisation energy, RNA-structure z-scores.
- Annotation-independent comparative genomics across 121 Myrtaceae plastomes,
  motif-vs-flank conservation, stem-loop covariation, CDS phylogeny.
- Whole-plastome synteny (MUMmer4) and Circos visualisations (pyCirclize):
  family-wide, tribe-representative and reference-genome comparisons.
- Publication figures (Times New Roman, editable SVG + 600 dpi JPEG).
- Removed the untrained CNN of the earlier draft.
